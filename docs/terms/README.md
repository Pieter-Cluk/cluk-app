# General terms and conditions

The texts of CLUK's general terms and conditions in four languages (`content_*.py`), reworked on 21 Oct 2026:
late-payment rule (1% per month, 15% penalty min €125 / max €3,500) moved into Article 7 without the old
"Bekafun" reference, payment immediately unless agreed per customer, quotes valid 30 days, courts of Ghent,
data protection chapter rewritten to match how CLUK works now. The English version prevails.

To change them: edit the texts, run `python3 build.py` here (needs Playwright with Chromium), and copy the PDFs to
`public/docs/` (file names in `config/rental.php`).
